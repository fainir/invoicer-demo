Privacy

Invoicer stores everything in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

  • Your business details, logo, clients and invoices are saved in this browser's local storage on this device only.
  • The app makes no network requests after it loads. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no cookies.
  • "Email invoice" opens your own mail app with a draft; Invoicer never sends email itself.
  • "Download backup" writes a JSON file to your device. Restoring it on another device copies your data there.
  • "Delete all data" in Settings removes everything Invoicer stored in this browser. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.

The source code is public at github.com/fainir/invoicer-demo.
